## Objective

Prevent collapsible Engine Observatory cards from stretching to the tallest grid item or growing indefinitely with dense diagnostic data.

## Scope

- Keep collapsed panels at their header height.
- Give expanded content a consistent 160 px minimum and 320 px maximum height.
- Make overflowing content independently vertically scrollable.
- Contain overscroll and reserve scrollbar space to reduce layout shift.
- Connect headers and content regions with accessible disclosure semantics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A collapsed card no longer stretches to match an expanded neighbor.
- Expanded card bodies remain between 160 px and 320 px tall.
- Dense content scrolls inside its own card without lengthening the whole grid row.
- Toggle buttons retain keyboard operation and expose their controlled detail region.
- Existing Observatory data and actions remain unchanged.
