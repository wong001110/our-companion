# UI-002 — Make PaperCard Tape Decoration Intentional on Operational Surfaces

## Status

In progress

## Objective

Make cream corner tape an explicit `PaperCard` decoration, remove repeated tape from Settings and Published Companion operational cards, and preserve the intentional narrative and Social page-level treatments.

## Base Commit

`12b715b2ab948dbb822606f3bb631484b76e1204`

## Branch

`main`

## Problem Evidence

- `PaperCard` accepts `tape`, but its root class does not expose that intent.
- `.paper-card::after` currently adds cream tape to every paper card.
- Settings operational cards opt into taped headings across Companion, AI, Voice, Privacy, Appearance, Online, Advanced, and Developer categories.
- Published Companion creates a second taped operational card inside Social's page-level taped card.
- The UI/UX specifications require opt-in tape, minimal operational decoration, and preservation of narrative treatments.

## In Scope

- Add a stable taped-state hook to `PaperCard` when `tape` is true.
- Apply the cream corner-tape pseudo-element only to explicitly taped cards.
- Preserve `paper-photo-card` decoration.
- Remove `tape` from Settings operational `PaperCard` usages.
- Remove `tape` from `PublishedCompanionSection`.
- Retain Social's single outer taped card.
- Retain Home, Journey, Memory, and other intentional narrative tape.
- Add focused component and Electron coverage.
- Capture English and Simplified Chinese evidence at normal and 760 px widths.

## Out of Scope

- Settings or Social redesign, navigation/category restructuring, or file splitting.
- Network, publishing, UI-001 friend lookup, AI, Voice, Privacy, Online, or form behavior changes.
- New variants, assets, typography, colors, tokens, or motion.
- `StickyNote`, notebook texture, photo-card visual behavior, Creation UI, server, or database changes.
- Broad refactoring.

- `PaperCard` exposes a taped-state class or equivalent only when `tape` is true.
- Plain `PaperCard` has no corner tape or taped section-label heading.
- Explicitly taped cards retain the intended treatment.
- `.paper-card::after` is not the unconditional selector.
- `paper-photo-card` remains unchanged.
- Settings operational cards and Published Companion are untaped without functional changes.
- Social retains at most one outer taped card and UI-001 remains correct.
- A representative Home card retains tape.
