## Objective

Make visible Companion Quick Action bubbles feel lightly suspended through subtle continuous motion after their entrance.

## Scope

- Add a gentle 4 px vertical float on a 2.6 second loop.
- Stagger each bubble's phase so the group moves organically rather than in unison.
- Isolate entrance, float, and hover/focus transforms so they compose without conflict.
- Disable continuous motion under Reduced Motion while preserving the existing opacity entrance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All four bubbles continue their existing entrance and then visibly drift up and down.
- Bubble phases differ across the group.
- Hover, focus, active state, clicks, placement, and interactive regions remain correct.
- Reduced Motion uses opacity only and has no continuous floating transform.
